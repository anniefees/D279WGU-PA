Dogs

Adopting Dogs: A Lifelong Companion

The domestic dog is an extremely social animal with over 300 recognized breeds. Adopting a dog offers mutual benefits for both the dog and the owner, improving social well-being and even physical health through reduced blood pressure and better lipid profiles. On this page, you will find information about how to care for your dogs throughout the various stages of their lives.

Diet & Exercise

Throughout a dog's life, they need to be fed and exercised appropriately. Puppies need more frequent meals, starting with nursing for the first two months. As they grow, their food intake can be reduced to one meal per day by the time they reach one year old. Regular exercise—between 30 to 120 minutes a day—helps maintain a healthy body and mind.

Grooming & Handling

Keeping your dog clean is crucial for its health. Regular brushing prevents shedding and matting, while periodic baths help eliminate dirt and pests. Use soaps formulated specifically for dogs to avoid skin irritation, and check for fleas and ticks regularly.

Vaccinations & Medications

Consult your veterinarian about core vaccines that protect against common diseases like rabies and parvovirus. Your vet may also recommend additional vaccinations based on your dog's environment, such as protection against Bordetella and Lyme disease.
