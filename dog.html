Dogs

Adopting Dogs: A Lifelong Companion

Adopting a dog offers mutual benefits for both the dog and the owner, improving social well-being and even physical health through reduced blood pressure and better lipid profiles. Learn how to care for dogs throughout the various stages of their lives.

Diet & Exercise

Puppies need more frequent meals, while adult dogs can be fed once per day. Regular exercise—between 30 to 120 minutes a day—helps maintain a healthy body and mind.

Grooming & Handling

Regular brushing prevents shedding and matting. Use soaps formulated specifically for dogs to avoid skin irritation, and check for fleas and ticks regularly.

Vaccinations & Medications

Core vaccines protect against diseases like rabies and parvovirus. Consult your vet for additional vaccinations based on your dog's environment.
