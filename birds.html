Birds

Understanding Your Bird's Needs

Birds are fascinating creatures, with species ranging from the tiny Cuban bee hummingbird to the massive ostrich. Each bird species has its own unique needs for social interaction, physical care, and mental well-being.

Birds and Mental Health

Birds can experience sadness and depression, just like humans. Common signs of depression in birds include reduced appetite, increased irritability, aggressive behavior, and changes in song tone. Consult a veterinarian if you notice these signs.
