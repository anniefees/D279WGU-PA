Birds

Understanding Your Bird's Needs

Birds are fascinating creatures, with species ranging from the tiny Cuban bee hummingbird to the massive ostrich. While many birds fly, others don’t. Each bird species has its own unique needs for social interaction, physical care, and mental well-being.

Birds and Mental Health

Birds can experience sadness and depression, just like humans. Common causes include illness, loss of a companion, or boredom. Signs of depression in birds include:

  • Reduced appetite
  • Increased irritability
  • Aggressive behavior
  • Change in song tone to a more solemn melody

If you notice these signs, consult a veterinarian. Early intervention can significantly improve your bird’s health and extend its life.
