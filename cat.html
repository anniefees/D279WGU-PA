Cats

The Journey of Cats

Cats were first domesticated around 7500 BCE. In this section, you'll find tips on caring for your cat at various stages of life.

From Birth to One Year

Kittens should nurse from their mother. By three to four weeks, wet kitten food is ideal for transitioning to solid food.

One to Seven Years: Adult Stage

As cats mature, their dietary needs change. Offering two meals a day helps maintain a healthy weight and prevents health issues.

Seven Years and Beyond: Senior Cats

As cats age, they need fewer calories and more high-quality protein. Be mindful of ingredient quality in your cat's food.
